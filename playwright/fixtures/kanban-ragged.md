## Middling [](?status=doing&nt_kanban_ordering_weight=2)

This card carries a couple of sentences, so it holds more text than its terse neighbour without running to the length of the wordy one. It is the median of the three, which is the card the column width is solved against.

## Wordy [](?status=doing&nt_kanban_ordering_weight=3)

This card carries several sentences of prose so that its text area is decisively larger than the median card's. Stacked, that extra area has to come out as extra width rather than as extra height, because a row is exactly one card tall and every card in it is solved against the same target height. Side by side the same difference would come out as a taller card instead, which is the transpose these three cards exist to demonstrate. The point of a third card is that a two-card lane makes the wordy one its own median, so it lands exactly on the floor and the widths cannot separate.
